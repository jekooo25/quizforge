TEACHER RESULTS — SEPARATE BATTLE AND WORD-BANK REPORTS

HOW TO OPEN
Teacher > Shared Reviewers > View student results.
For a closed quiz, use List of Reviewers > View student results.
Use All modes, Quiz Battle or Word Bank to filter reports.
Refresh after a student finishes or sends saved results.

WHAT IS SHOWN
Student name and review mode.
First-attempt correct count, total and accuracy.
Practice recall correct count and attempts, separately.
Remaining review items, their original question and source page when available.
Completion date in UTC+8 and the latest practice-update date.
Battle points and victory/defeat apply only to Quiz Battle.
Word-bank reports never award battle points, Stars or task answers.

WORD-BANK DELIVERY
Only quizzes joined from the laptop sharing server are reported to teachers.
Finishing a word-bank round saves a report for delivery.
Practise missed updates that SAME round's practice section.
First-attempt accuracy stays unchanged; a New round creates a separate attempt.
If the connection fails, results remain in the student outbox.
Student > Shared quizzes > Send saved results retries the latest saved snapshot.
Duplicate delivery does not create duplicate reports.

SCENES TO EDIT
scenes/TeacherResults.tscn
  Header — back and refresh buttons
  QuizTitle, Status — reviewer and connection information
  Filters — three mode filters
  Scroll/Sections/BattleSection/BattleList — battle report cards
  Scroll/Sections/SpellSection/SpellList — word-bank report cards

scenes/TeacherResultCard.tscn
  Student, Mode, FirstPass, Recall, Score, Completed, Updated, Remaining
  All permanent card labels are editable in the scene tree.
  The controller instances this scene per completed round; it does not construct labels in code.

scenes/SpellResults.tscn
  Delivery — student report-delivery message

DATABASE COMPATIBILITY
The sharing service adds report fields to its existing SQLite database.
Earlier reports remain classified as Quiz Battle.
Earlier reports without per-question details say those details were not recorded.
No earlier scores, publications or joins are deleted.

IMPORTANT
Restart the laptop sharing-server window after installing this update.
This remains a laptop-only demonstration with local role selection, not secure online accounts.
Scores are reported by the game client; this prototype does not verify them against cheating.
