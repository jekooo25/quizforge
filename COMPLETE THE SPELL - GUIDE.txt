COMPLETE THE SPELL — WORD-BANK REVIEW MODE

HOW TO OPEN
Play Quizzes > REVIEW on a saved quiz > Start Word Bank.
REVIEW opens the editable mode picker; Start Quiz Battle opens the multiple-choice battle.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap a word to insert it into the missing part of the sentence.
Tap Cast Spell to submit it.
Correct answers animate and advance automatically.
Incorrect answers show the completed source sentence, explanation and reviewer page.
Take your time reading, then tap Continue.
At the end, use Practise missed for remaining sentences or New round for another short round.
Pause freezes cast resolution and automatic advancement.

WHICH QUIZZES WORK
The mode uses the saved reviewer quote, not a guessed or invented sentence.
A usable question needs four distinct short answer choices, a source quote and a source page.
The correct answer must appear exactly once as a whole word or phrase in that quote.
Quotes with a repeated answer are skipped to avoid giving the answer away elsewhere.
If there are no suitable sentences, the mode explains why and offers a return to My Quizzes.
Generate or review a source-supported quiz to add more suitable material.

SCENES YOU CAN EDIT IN GODOT
scenes/CompleteTheSpell.tscn
  Layout/Column/Header — navigation, title, progress, spell count, pause
  Challenge — existing Emberpup, spell effect and power indicator
  Challenge/Board/BoardScroll — sentence and source/explanation review
  WordBank/Words — four editable word buttons
  CastButton, ContinueButton, AutoBar — progression controls
  Unavailable — no suitable source sentences message
  Results — instance of scenes/SpellResults.tscn
  PauseMenu — instance of scenes/SpellPauseMenu.tscn (inherits the shared pause layout)

scenes/SpellResults.tscn
  First-pass accuracy, practice recall and remaining review items.
  Practise missed, New round and My Quizzes buttons.

The permanent UI is in scene files. Scripts supply question data, signals and state changes.

SCORING
Spell counts belong to the current review round.
This mode does not change battle scores, Stars or Daily Tasks.
Quizzes joined from the laptop server send a separate word-bank report to the teacher.
Practise missed updates that round without overwriting first-attempt accuracy.
First-pass accuracy remains separate from subsequent word-bank practice recall.
A correct practice answer does not claim long-term mastery.

CURRENT LIMITS
Tap-to-select word bank only; typed answers are not included.
Source-supported cards are derived locally from the selected saved quiz.
No new AI request or running server is needed to play this mode.
Real Android cutouts and gestures still need physical-device testing.
